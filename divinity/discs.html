Recipes

All seven are shapeless crafting recipes in the divinity_music_discs group. The shared base is an amethyst shard, an echo shard, a disc fragment 5, a diamond, and a dye — with three exceptions called out below:

DiscIngredients
White FerrariAmethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, white dye
Death MetalAmethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, black dye
Coffee BeanAmethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, brown dye
One MoreAmethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, yellow dye
WoodsOak log (instead of amethyst shard), echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, green dye
黒い&#涙;Amethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, crying obsidian, black dye
MajulaAmethyst shard, echo shard, disc fragment 5, diamond, nether star, emerald, gold ingot

Sound setup

  • Seven sound events (music.whiteferrari through music.majula), each pointing at its streamed .ogg file (stream: true).
  • Each track has its own subtitle line (e.g. “White Ferrari plays”, “Death Metal rumbles”).
  • Item names, track descriptions, and jukebox titles are localised in English and Italian.
