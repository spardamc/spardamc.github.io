Listening experience

Built on the vanilla jukebox, with a persistent overlay so you always know what is playing.

On this page
  1. Jukebox playback
  2. Now Playing HUD
  3. Creative tab

Jukebox playback

The discs play through the vanilla jukebox — each disc is jukebox-playable and bound to its jukebox song, so comparator outputs, track lengths, and song titles all behave like vanilla discs. See the track table for lengths and comparator values.

Now Playing HUD

While a Divinity track is active, a persistent Now Playing overlay renders at the top centre of the screen, below the boss bar:

  • Shows the current song title (already in “Divinity - X” form via localisation).
  • A progress bar under the text tracks playback against the song's length.
  • It respects hidden GUI (nothing renders with GUI hidden) and clears when the track ends.

A client-side tracker follows the jukebox song player — when a Divinity song starts, the HUD picks up its title and duration; when it stops, the overlay clears.

Creative tab

All seven discs live in a dedicated Divinity creative tab, using the White Ferrari disc as its icon. Item names, track descriptions, jukebox titles, and subtitles are localised in English and Italian.
