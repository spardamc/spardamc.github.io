Artifacts & roadmap

Everything on this page is roadmap, not status: planned work described in the mod's own planning doc, not finished features.

Planned artifacts

Not yet in the build Only the seven discs are registered items today. The names below exist in asset or language files, or in the roadmap — none of them is a working item yet.

The roadmap plans unique divine items with active or passive behaviours — item component APIs or event listeners where specialised rendering or particle effects are needed — implemented after the disc registries. It names an amulet among them. Names already present in asset or language files but not registered as items include the Violastro and the Black Vinyl (a violastro/black vinyl texture is also called out in the asset plan). Treat all of these as reserved names for future artifacts, not as content you can obtain.

Item acquisition is also still being designed: craftable recipes and acquisition methods via data generation or custom JSON recipe files are a roadmap step, as is registering further item groups or creative tabs.

The five steps

  1. Environment & mod initialisation — Fabric on Minecraft 1.21.11 with Java 21, mod metadata (mod id divinity), entrypoints, and the fabric-api dependency.
  2. Asset integration — textures under assets/divinity/textures/, item model JSON files, and .ogg tracks wired through the sounds.json registry.
  3. Item & sound registries — custom sound events, the custom RecordItem discs, and English/Italian localisations for item names and jukebox track titles.
  4. Custom logic & data generation — behaviours for the divine items, recipes and acquisition methods, item groups and creative tabs. Roadmap.
  5. Testing, build & integration — local client verification (item textures, sound playback, crash-free compatibility with Sodium and Iris), then compiling the standalone Fabric jar. Roadmap.

Planned client work

  • Jukebox song tooltips.
  • Particle and rendering effects for divine items.
  • Sodium and Iris compatibility verification.
