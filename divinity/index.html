Divinity Music

A focused expansion for Minecraft's audio experience: seven custom music discs with streamed tracks, jukebox songs, crafting recipes, a dedicated creative tab, and a persistent Now Playing HUD while a track is active.

In development Minecraft 1.21.1 Fabric Java 21
Not yet released Divinity Music is in development and has no download yet. What is described here as built reflects the current source; everything else is marked as roadmap.

What is roadmap

Artifacts

Divine items with active or passive behaviours — planned, not yet in the build. The roadmap names an amulet among them.

Artifacts & roadmap →

The mod's own planning doc is a five-step roadmap — environment and metadata, asset integration, item and sound registries, custom logic and data generation, then testing, build, and integration. The registry and asset steps are what you see described here; behaviours, acquisition tuning, and release packaging come later.
