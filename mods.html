Scripta

Available now v1.0.0 Fabric 1.21.1
Download on CurseForge

A data-driven global questing mod for Minecraft 1.21.1, currently shipping on Fabric. Quests are authored once as JSON data, loaded like advancements, and identical across every world and server — players only carry progress state.

  • Five objective types — break blocks, kill entities, collect items, reach custom stats, grant advancements — each with its own required count.
  • Eleven reward types — items, XP, XP levels, commands, advancements, toasts, weighted tables, loot crates, and interactive player choices.
  • Weighted reward tables — roll one prize, grant them all, or hand over a physical loot crate the player opens themselves.
  • Two editors — a browser-based web editor with a tokenised operator link, plus a full in-game editor behind operator level 2.
  • Per-player progress — completion, objective counters, and claimed rewards persist per player in world SavedData and sync on join.
  • Themeable quest book — per-pane surfaces, colours, sizes, and widget styles, including custom background textures.

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric Loader 0.16.9+, Fabric API, and Architectury API. OwoLib ships inside the Scripta jar — do not install it separately.

Florarium

In development Fabric 1.21.1

A magic-tech mod built around a mana economy and a 28-bloom flower progression chain. One unlimited Mana Pool anchors everything: daylight, fuels, XP, and flower generators feed it, while crafting stations and spell casts drain it. The flower chain is the tech tree — each bloom crafts the next via the Bloom Apothecary, from Ember Wick through ring rites and the forge tier to the Prism Bloom capstone (2000 mana/s with a Luck aura).

Around the chain: six crafting and utility stations with hopper automation, tiered Bonsai Miners (T1–T4 ore generation with GUI, auto-smelt, and network routing), garden layout rules that punish monoculture, Prism Ore mining deep underground (y −48 to −8), and a custom particle FX framework. The Bloom Magic 2.0 spell engine — API interfaces, wand management, and a legacy mana bridge that drains pool mana per spell tier — is still being built, as are the Bonsai Farmer and Glowing Ore blocks.

Divinity Music

In development Fabric 1.21.1

A focused expansion for Minecraft's audio experience. Seven custom music discs — White Ferrari (4:09), Death Metal (4:22), Coffee Bean (3:29), One More (1:39), Woods (4:46), 黒い&#涙; (5:07), Majula (3:19) — each a Rare single-stack jukebox-playable item with a streamed .ogg track, its own jukebox song and comparator output, a shapeless crafting recipe, English and Italian localisations, and per-track subtitles. A dedicated Divinity creative tab holds them all, and a persistent Now Playing HUD shows the current track with a progress bar while it plays through a vanilla jukebox.

Next on the roadmap: divine artifacts with active or passive behaviours (an amulet is named among them), plus recipe and acquisition tuning, client tooltips and particles, and Sodium/Iris compatibility verification. Names like Violastro and Black Vinyl exist in assets but are not working items yet.
