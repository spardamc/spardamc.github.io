Our mods

Scripta

A data-driven global questing mod for Minecraft 1.21.1: author quests once as JSON, track objectives with prerequisites, and hand out rewards from items and XP to commands, weighted tables, and interactive choices — all in a themeable in-game quest book with a browser-based editor.

Available now · v1.0.0

Start here: Scripta docs → Download on CurseForge →

Florarium

A magic-tech mod built around a mana economy and a 28-bloom flower progression chain. Generate mana with flowers, spend it at crafting stations like the Bloom Apothecary to grow the next bloom, and automate ore with tiered Bonsai Miners — with the Bloom Magic 2.0 spell engine still being built.

In development · no download yet

Our Philosophy

We believe that Minecraft mods should feel like a natural extension of the game. Whether it's a quest system that integrates with your favorite UI or a magic mod that respects the vanilla aesthetic, Sparda focuses on stability, performance, and high-quality design.

Looking for help? Scripta troubleshooting lives on our Support page with the full Scripta FAQ alongside. For Florarium and Divinity Music, which are unreleased, the support page explains what to do while you wait.
