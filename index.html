We build for Minecraft.

Sparda is a small collective of developers and artists creating deeply integrated, data-driven, and visually polished experiences for the Fabric ecosystem. Our released work is Scripta — a global questing mod with full documentation and long-term support.

Meet our mod Meet the team

What we build

We make mods that feel like a natural extension of the game: systems that server operators can configure as plain data, interfaces that respect the vanilla aesthetic, and behaviour that stays predictable in production. Scripta is that philosophy shipped — author quests once as JSON, track objectives with prerequisites, and hand out rewards from items and XP to commands, weighted tables, and interactive choices, all inside a themeable in-game quest book with a browser-based editor.

Our quality bar

Stability first

Malformed content is skipped with a log line, never a crash. Per-player progress persists in world SavedData and syncs on join.

Data over code

Operators and pack authors work in plain JSON and live editors — no mod recompiles, no in-world node graphs.

Vanilla feel

Interfaces and art direction stay close to Minecraft's own look, themeable where it counts.

Documented and supported

Every feature ships with reference docs and troubleshooting. See the Scripta FAQ or our Support page.
