Bloom chain

The flower chain is the tech tree: 28 blooms, each crafting the next via the Bloom Apothecary. Numbers below are exact — mana costs and craft times (20 ticks = 1 second) come from the source.

Stages 0–1: first sparks

Craft a Mana Pool (stone bricks) and place it — everything else needs pool mana nearby. Then craft an Ember Wick (tall, 2 blocks) to start generating, and Apothecary-craft a Petal Rain (Ember Wick + seeds, 100 mana), which boosts crops and drops your first essence.

The Bloom Apothecary needs open sky and crafts from your inventory. In order:

The Wand of Binding is the ritual tool (bind/trigger modes, toggled with shift-right-click). The Architect Wand raises a complete rite circle — 1 Apothecary + 4 pedestals — in one click, consuming the blocks from the player's inventory.

Stage 2: ring rites

Build the ring — 1 Apothecary + 4 Bloom Pedestals placed 2 blocks out N/S/E/W (or Architect-click it) — then right-click the Apothecary with a Bloom Pearl to attune it, carry a Rune of Copper (copper block + iron + lapis), and trigger rites with the binding wand in trigger mode:

Stage 3: forge tier

The forge tier runs on the Apothecary: attune with a Bloom Diamond, keep lava within 4 blocks (or build in the Nether), carry a Rune of Cinder, and trigger with the binding wand. Jobs take real minutes and pop out on top; the burn visibly intensifies near completion.

Stage 4: awakened blooms

  • Elder Bloom (right-click, 1000 mana): reveals nearest village coordinates.
  • Genesis Bloom (hold a bloom flower, 2500 mana): clones it, 20-minute cooldown.
  • Diamond Bloom (right-click, 5000 pool mana): forges a Mana-Charged Upgrade.
  • Seasonal Bloom: mass-grows crops in a 64-block range (8 mana each).
  • Bloomheart Core: grants nearby players Absorption + Regen (8 mana each).
  • Twilight Bloom: doubles nearby generation at full moon.
  • Wayfinder Bud (side craft: compass + map + elder essence): faint ping toward the nearest village.
  • Hearthglow (side craft: campfire + potato + seasonal essence): feeds hungry players nearby (Saturation, 4 mana each).

Essences

Essences drop from most flowers. Four of one essence plus 50 mana at the Essence Condenser becomes Condensed Essence — the key to the condensed flowers and the Bloomheart Core. Side and utility flowers drop none.

Prism Ore and the capstone

Late-late game lives underground: Prism Ore veins spawn deep (y −48 to −8, diamond pickaxe required) and drop Prism Shards (fortune-friendly). Four shards plus a Genesis Bloom buy the Prism Bloom — 2000 mana a second with a Luck aura, the strongest generator in the mod.
