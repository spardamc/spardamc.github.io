Mana economy

Mana is the single resource everything drinks from. One unlimited pool, many generators, and layout rules that punish lazy gardening.

The Mana Pool

The Mana Pool is the only storage, and it is unlimited. A balance of 50,000 reads as “full” for the visible water level (0–8 fill states), bonuses, and conversions — but it never caps.

  • Flowers and machines drink from the nearest pool within 8 blocks.
  • Touching pools share one bank (a connected-component network, up to 128 blocks).
  • Daylight trickles 6 mana/s into sky-open pools.

Feeding the pool

Beyond flowers and daylight, the pool accepts direct offerings:

  • Fuel items thrown in for mana — e.g. lava bucket 4000, coal block 3600, blaze rod 600.
  • XP conversion: 1 level → 1000 mana, with a 5-minute cooldown per player.
  • Attunement conversions: ender pearls and diamonds thrown near a sufficiently full pool auto-convert to Bloom Pearls (1000 mana) and Bloom Diamonds (2500 mana).

Spending and reach

Stations and auras drink pool mana within 8 blocks. To feed distant farms, chain Mana Relays: touch one to a pool (any side), chain more outward, and right-click a relay to read the mana it carries.

Garden layout rules

  • Same-flower neighbours halve output each (up to 6 halvings) — spread gardens out.
  • Duskbell boosts nearby night output ×1.25.
  • Diamond Bloom adds +10% generation nearby.
  • Twilight Bloom doubles nearby generation at full moon.
  • Fertilizer splash: a 3-minute buff — double mana, +30% essence chance, halved cooldowns.

Spell casting draws mana

Through the legacy mana bridge, every spell cast drains pool mana by tier: T1 costs 20, T2 costs 60, T3/T4 cost 150, and T5+ costs 250. Florarium ships no wands or spells of its own — install Wizards (RPG Series) alongside, and its spells draw from your pools through this bridge. See Bloom Magic & FX for the engine status.
