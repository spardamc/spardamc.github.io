Bloom Apothecary & rite circle

The Bloom Apothecary is the primary crafting station (needs open sky), with 15 recipes across 3 tiers (base, rite, forge). It crafts from the player's inventory, supports hand-craft and auto-craft, and auto-fires when hopper-fed while pool mana holds.

Deeper tiers need the rite circle: 4 Bloom Pedestals placed 2 blocks out N/S/E/W from the Apothecary. The circle unlocks rite recipes (Frostbloom through Rune Bloom) and forge recipes (Cinder Bloom through Bloomheart Core). The Architect Wand raises the complete circle in one click, consuming the blocks from the inventory.

Two wands orchestrate it all: the Wand of Binding is the ritual tool with bind and trigger modes (shift-right-click to toggle), and the Architect Wand is the one-click builder.

Bonsai Miners

Tiered single-block ore generators (T1–T4). Right-click opens a GUI to cycle ore, toggle auto-smelt, and view progress and mana status. Growth slows to 10% when no pool is in range (mana-starved state). Adjacent miners form a miner network whose outputs route to adjacent inventories via the Fabric Transfer API. Speeds are configurable via divinity_additions.json.

Automation

  • Hoppers push ingredients into the Apothecary, Condenser, Forge, and ring from any side or top; finished goods exit the bottom.
  • Breaking a station drops its buffer — nothing voids.
  • Pedestals take and give hopper IO for display stock.
  • The things hoppers cannot do: attune, stoke, and trigger — those stay player gestures by design.
